# Re-architecting the five systems into Holon — the final plan

**Written 2026-10-23T23:22Z.** This is the third revision, and it supersedes the two
before it. Those plans ported machinery and then ported a KB architecture. The Chair
corrected both: **we have our own genome KB and a new architecture, so nothing is
retro-fitted, and we re-architect instead.**

The instruction that frames everything below: *"beginning from re-architecting
everything, not trying to fix the sphere in the cube."*

---

## 1. The old plan was solving the wrong problem, and here is the proof

`2026-10-23-five-pool-import-plan.md` tried to import 48 cron protocols into 14
routines. `2026-10-23-port-architecture-core-and-information.md` then tried to map the
corpus's JSON knowledge base onto `holon memory`, and spent a whole section on whether
a score adjustment should be a `decision` or a raw JSON blob.

**That second question was generated by the wrong premise.** The corpus's KB is
~3,000 loose JSON files, three 0-byte sqlite files, a 527-line hand-rolled runtime
importing from outside the corpus, and 301 research files of which only 14 are
distinct. Mapping that onto any primitive is retrofitting.

While checking the terrain I found what the OS already ships. `holon/vendor/genome.py`
carries **five built-in blueprints**, and one of them is called **`venture-pipeline`**:

> *"the three lanes that turn a signal into paid work (job, freelance, architecture).
> Each lane keeps its own ladder, because the rules differ (a lead needs a named
> person; an application does not). One append-only outcome ledger, shared by all
> three, is the only input to the lane score."*

It declares `application`, `gig`, `lead`, `contact` and an append-only `outcome`
ledger, with states, transitions, typed relations, `evidence_on` gates, and
`human_only` on every win. And its playbook closes with the mapping from the very
systems in front of us:

> *"career-pipeline `application_statuses` → `application` states, one to one…
> architectural-leads items with no named person stay `trigger`; the 301 current items
> are triggers, not leads."*

**The re-architecture is already built.** `career-pipeline`, `freelance-agency` and
`architectural-leads` are the three lanes of a blueprint that predates this survey.
`community-loo` and `credibility-funnel` are the other two blueprints (`ontology`,
`literature-research`). Nothing is being forced into a cube; the cube was built for
this sphere.

## 2. What the new architecture actually is

Read from `docs/INTENT.md` and `docs/BYLAWS.md`, not from the legacy corpus:

**An organization, not a pipeline.** Chair → CEO → departments → employees with
persistent identity, roles, memory scope, a record and a manager. Six roles:
head, researcher, planner, executor, verifier, steward — each performing stages of a
pipeline (`shape, approve, research, plan, execute, verify, retro`).

**Knowledge lives in two places, both native:**

| Primitive | What it is | When it is used |
|---|---|---|
| `holon memory` | scoped items with 8 kinds, FTS5, one writer, provenance, promotion from emp → dept → org | distilled, current knowledge an agent should carry |
| `holon kb` | **one git repository per KB, with typed kinds, a lifecycle, states, transitions, typed relations, a `wake` view, and one commit per write** | a domain with a lifecycle and a history |

**The agent's context is compiled, not scraped.** `compile_context` builds the brief
in a fixed order: your role → the task → the playbook → stage material → the approved
plan and its rubric → the gold standard (the last verified result of this job) → the
KBs' `genome wake` cut → cross-scope recall. `holon recall` answers a question across
memory, episodes, skills, playbooks and verified results in one call.

So: **a KB is not a file store with a search index. It is a typed graph with a
lifecycle whose state machine the kernel enforces.** `evidence_on` means a state move
needs evidence; `human_only` means a person confirms it. That is the entire difference,
and it is what the legacy architecture could not express — 3,000 JSON files with a
status field and no transitions between them.

**What this means for tokens and tool calls, concretely.** The legacy systems' cost
was per-file: read a JSON, parse it, decide, write a JSON, update a state file. The
blueprints' answer is per-command: `genome new --batch -` ingests one whole source's
worth of claims in a single call; `genome show CL-n` reads exactly what is needed and
nothing else; `genome find` / `view` / `graph` answer questions; `genome wake` is a
free orientation that lands in the agent's brief through the KB section of the
bundle. **The lean path is not a cleverer way to read the corpus — it is to stop
reading the corpus**, and let the kernel compile the brief.

**And the loop closes.** The blueprint's `playbook` text is the protocol an employee
reads; the OS's own playbooks are *versioned department skills refined from verified
outcomes* (`capabilities/playbooks.py`). So the operating rules these five systems
discovered over 2026-08 to 2026-10 — the 42 ADs and 34 RGs — become the thing the
next run is graded against, and improve from the outcome ledger. That is what
`compound_learning.json` was a hand-maintained imitation of.

## 3. Where each of the five systems lands

| System | Blueprint | What it becomes | What it does **not** become |
|---|---|---|---|
| **career-pipeline** | `venture-pipeline` | `application` items + `outcome` rows; one **Recruitment** department; an applicant employee per stage | 48 cron protocols, 169 scripts, a 43M JSON tree |
| **freelance-agency** | `venture-pipeline` | `gig` items + `outcome` rows; one **Delivery** department | the same 172 scripts, the `*-loop-p*` cron family |

**Three of the five already exist as first-class kinds.** Two map to domain-knowledge
blueprints. **No custom KB architecture is imported, and no legacy ladder is retrofitted
— the blueprints' own ladders are the target, and the corpus's ladders map onto them.**

## 5. The migration, phased, information first

**Phase 0 — the Chair's rulings (no code).** Two: which systems become departments
(given five pools and three lanes, the natural answer is **one department per lane
plus two for the domain knowledge**), and the KB naming. §7.5 of the survey holds the
options with measured costs. Nothing has been ratified.

**Phase 1 — the records.** 42 ADs and 34 RGs into `docs/decisions/` and
`docs/regressions/`, supersession edges preserved. `LO-AD-001` is amended in part by
`LO-AD-002 §1`, and flattening that loses the thing these documents are best at. Verify
by reading back through `genome show`, not by diffing files.

**Phase 2 — the department, one pool, with the pipeline empty.** Seed the
`venture-pipeline` blueprint for one lane, create the department, hire the employees
into the six roles, fork the SKILL.md in. **No data yet.** Prove the shape works: a
`application` moves `discovered → applied` and the transition refuses for want of an
`outcome` row, and `closed-won` refuses for want of a human.

**Phase 3 — the outcome ledger, re-expressed from the two real ledgers.**
`career-pipeline/kb/applications.jsonl` (56 rows) and
`freelance-agency/kb/decisions/*.jsonl` (17,510 rows) → append-only `outcome` rows.
This is the lane score's only input and the highest-value artifact in the port: it is
the part that makes the organization smarter about itself. **The per-item
`kb/decisions/*.json` files are not a source** — their `outcome` is a score, 0 of 116
map, and importing them would corrupt the score.

**What the ledger already says, once it is in the blueprint's shape.** The corpus's own
decisions.jsonl records **4,289 `no_reply`** against 229 `proposal_sent` — that ratio
is the freelance lane's actual conversion signal, and it is invisible today because
nothing counts it. The blueprint's lane score exists to compute exactly this
(P(first paid reply within 30 days) × first ticket ÷ days to cash, per lane). **The
single most valuable thing this migration produces is that number**, and it needs no
new architecture: only the rows in the shape the score script already reads.

**Phase 4 — the live items, by state.** Only the items the corpus itself says are
live. `architectural-leads`' 301 become `trigger`, not leads — the blueprint's rule is
the schema's, not a migration decision.

**Phase 5 — the routines, recreated by lane, not ported by cron.** `holon routine add`
with the classifier choosing script/fast/full. **The 12 `*-loop-p*` twins are not
created**, and the two cadence refusals (`reddit-karma-lane-2h-draft-post` 12/day,
`dad-arch-strategist-listener` 26/day) are the guard working and are preserved as
records, not worked around.

**Phase 6 — retire the machinery, per pool, only after the department runs it.**
Nothing is deleted; the corpus is the archive of record.

## 6. The blockers, ranked, with what closes each

1. **Phase 0 is unanswered**, and it now gates only the department topology, nothing
   else. Closed by the Chair's ruling; every other phase can be *built* before it.
2. **No live-work guard exists.** 14 rows of live work sit behind the skip and the
   registry snapshot is 4 days stale. Closed by re-copying `routine-latest.json` before
   Phase 4 and by reading it per row, not trusting the corpus's `status:`.
3. **The outcome ledger exists, but not where I first looked — and one tempting
   source is a trap.** The blueprint says the outcome ledger is the lane score's only
   input. Measured, the ledger is **two** places, both append-only: `career-pipeline/kb/
   applications.jsonl` (56 rows, `submitted|failed|needs-human`) and
   `freelance-agency/kb/decisions/*.jsonl` (17,510 rows across daily files, whose own
   vocabulary maps onto the blueprint's `sent|failed|reply`). So the highest-value
   artifact **is** portable, with no invention.

   **The trap:** the per-item `kb/decisions/*.json` files (116) look like the ledger
   and are not. Every one carries an `outcome` value, and **0 of 116 are a blueprint
   outcome state** — they are scores (`scored_medium` 47, `scored_low` 47, `scored`
   20). Importing those as `outcome` rows would fill the lane score's only input with
   116 non-events and make the score meaningless. Closed by mapping from the two
   jsonl ledgers and leaving the per-item files as archive.
4. **`community-loo` and `credibility-funnel` have no blueprint that names them** the
   way `venture-pipeline` names the three lanes. They fit `ontology` and
   `literature-research` by shape, not by declaration. **That inference is the
   weakest claim in this document** and is Phase 0's third question, not my call.
5. **No measured baseline for token cost.** The claim needs a number: one old job end
   to end, then the same job on the graph. `kb/runs/` holds the old system's own
   records.

## 7. What this document does not claim

- **Nothing has been seeded, created or applied.** No department, no KB, no routine,
  no entity, no `genome new`. All read-only.
- **The blueprints have been read, not exercised.** `venture-pipeline`'s states and
  transitions were read from source; its behaviour under an actual `genome new` /
  `genome move` is Phase 2's first measurement. In particular I have not run the
  blueprint against a real corpus item, so *"the schema refuses `contacted` without a
  person"* is the blueprint's declared rule, not an observed refusal.
- **I have not read all 76 core records** — one in full, two front-matters.
- **The three-lane-to-department mapping is my reading, not the Chair's.** The
  blueprint declares three lanes; it does not say how they map onto departments.
- **The 17,510 freelance rows are counted, not classified.** I measured the
  `decision` vocabulary and proposed a mapping to the blueprint's `sent|failed|reply`
  states; the mapping itself is a Phase 3 decision, and `no_reply` in particular is a
  judgement call — it is absence of a reply, which the blueprint's ledger has no
  single state for.
- **`community-loo` and `credibility-funnel` are mapped by shape, not by declaration.**
  See blocker 4. This is the weakest claim in the document.
